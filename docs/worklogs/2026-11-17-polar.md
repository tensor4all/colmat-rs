# Polar decomposition (colmat-rs#60, PR 2)

## Decisions

- **API: MatrixAlgebraKit.jl's conventions.** The issue names a "MatrixAlgebraKit Rust port" for
  the API; none was found in the tensor4all organisation, so the conventions are those of
  MatrixAlgebraKit.jl (`src/interface/polar.jl`, main at `6fddde30`): `left_polar(A) -> W, P` with
  `A = W P` for `m >= n`, `right_polar(A) -> P, Wᴴ` with `A = P Wᴴ` for `m <= n`. In faer style
  they take explicit outputs (`W`/`P`, `P`/`Wh`), a `MemStack` sized by `left_polar_scratch` /
  `right_polar_scratch`, `Par`, and `PolarParams` (`method`). `P` is stored in full and is
  exactly hermitian (real diagonal), so it can be passed on without a triangle convention.
- **`right_polar` is `left_polar` of `A^T`, read transposed**: `A^T = W' P'` gives
  `A = P'^T W'^T`, and `P'^T = conj(P')` is hermitian positive semidefinite. No second code path.
- **Two methods, as the issue asks.** `PolarMethod::Svd` (default and reference) is
  `W = U V^H`, `P = V Σ V^H` from the thin SVD. `PolarMethod::Qdwh` uses only QR, Cholesky and
  matrix products. Newton-Schulz is left out (the issue allows a follow-up).
- **QDWH written from the papers, not from code.** Nakatsukasa, Bai and Gygi (2010) for the
  weights, the QR form of the step and the stopping rule; Nakatsukasa and Higham (2012) for column
  pivoting in the QR form; Nakatsukasa and Higham (2013) for switching to the Cholesky form once
  `c_k <= 100`. MatrixFactorizations.jl's `polar.jl` was read only for its interface (docstring,
  `polar` entry point, `SVDAlg`), not for its QDWH code; its output is the test oracle. No code is
  translated, so `NOTICE` gains no entry. Details:
  - a tall `A` is first reduced to its `n x n` triangular factor (unpivoted QR); `W = Q W_R`;
  - `X_0 = R / ||R||_F` and the weights start from the lower bound `l_0 = u` on its smallest
    singular value instead of an estimate of `σ_min`. This costs no condition estimate and fixes
    the iteration count at six (`f64`) / four (`f32`) whatever the conditioning; an estimate of
    `σ_min` would save one or two steps on well-conditioned input and is not done;
  - stop when `||X_{k+1} - X_k||_F <= (5u)^{1/3}` and `1 - l_{k+1} <= 10u`; at most 20 steps,
    then `PolarError::NoConvergence` (reached only with non-finite input; the Cholesky of
    `I + c X^H X` also reports it);
  - `P` is the hermitian part of `W^H A`;
  - a subnormal (or `norm_l2`-underflowed) `||A||_F` is first scaled by `1 / eps`, an exact power
    of two, because its reciprocal overflows.
- **Column pivoting in the QR step is required, not optional.** With an unpivoted QR of
  `[sqrt(c) X; I]` the first steps (`sqrt(c) ≈ 5e10` in `f64`) gave an orthonormal `W` that is not
  the polar factor: on the 33 x 33 `sample(i, j)` matrix (condition 5.4e9),
  `||A - W P||_F / ||A||_F = 5e-10` and `W^H A` was not hermitian (relative defect 1e-9). The same
  iteration in Julia with LAPACK's unpivoted QR gives the same 5e-10, and with `qr(B, ColumnNorm())`
  4.8e-16 (also at 31 x 31 and 40 x 40), so this is the algorithm, not a faer kernel. Using the
  exact `σ_min` as `l_0` only reduced it to 5e-13. `Q_1 Q_2^H` is a block of the projector
  `Q Q^H`, so the pivoting does not change the step. MatrixFactorizations.jl's `:qdwh` gives
  7.7e-16 on the same matrix.
- **What QDWH does not guarantee**: `W` has orthonormal columns only when `σ_min(A) >= u ||A||_F`.
  Below that the iteration does not reach the null space (columns of `W` there are not
  orthonormal, zero for a zero column of `A`); `A = W P` and `P >= 0` still hold. Documented on
  `PolarMethod::Qdwh`. The SVD method has no such limit.
- **No LAPACK validation family, no `lapack-names.md` row**: LAPACK has no polar decomposition
  routine, so there is no checker to port and no routine name to map.

## Verification conclusions and constraints

- `faer/tests/polar.rs` (7 tests) for f32, f64, c32, c64: `A = W P` / `A = P W^H` within
  `200 max(m, n) eps ||A||_F`, `W` orthonormal within `200 max(m, n) eps`, `P` exactly hermitian
  with `λ_min(P) >= -200 max(m, n) eps ||A||_F`, and, for full rank, QDWH against SVD within
  `200 max(m, n) eps cond(A)` (`W`) and `200 max(m, n) eps ||A||_F` (`P`). Inputs: empty, 1 x 1
  to 100 x 33 and their transposes, zero, rank-deficient (repeated column, zero column), graded
  columns to condition `eps^{-4/5}`, the nearly singular `sample(i, j)` matrices, and scales
  `min_positive / eps` and `max_positive / 64`. Outputs start as garbage and are views into
  guarded buffers; row-major input; exact scratch for every call; `Par::Seq` against a two-thread
  team (257 x 129, 129 x 257, 129 x 129, f64 and c64) within the same bounds.
- Against MatrixFactorizations.jl at `a38f07fbbcb8abebb203bf67fc745b8c262d6d19` (`alg = :svd`,
  cross-checked by the generator against `:qdwh`): f64 and c64, 5 x 3, 4 x 4, 3 x 5 (through
  `polar(A')`, as the package only takes `m >= n`) and a 6 x 4 matrix of condition 1e8; both
  methods agree entry by entry within the bounds above. Generator:
  `faer/tests/fixtures/polar_reference/generate.jl`.
- `faer/tests/polar_no_alloc.rs`: no heap allocation in a warmed-up `Par::Seq` call, both
  methods, left and right, f64 and c64.
- Mutation checks, each reverted: an unpivoted QR step fails
  `polar_qdwh_backward_stable_nearly_singular` (residual 4.8e-9 against a bound of 1.4e-11);
  dropping the hermitian part of the QDWH `P` fails five tests; storing `P'` instead of its
  transpose in `right_polar` fails five tests, including the MatrixFactorizations.jl comparison.
- The one new absolute term is in `polar_qdwh_subnormal_norm`: `64 * 2^-1074` added to the
  residual bound, for the rounding of subnormal entries of `P` and `W P`; the relative part is
  unchanged.
- **Inherited, near the underflow threshold (recorded only; upstream not measured).** On a 6 x 4
  `f64` matrix scaled by `f64::MIN_POSITIVE`, the thin SVD reconstructs with relative residual
  0.79 (one singular value returned as 0) and the unpivoted QR with 1.0; the SVD still gives
  0.08 at `4 * MIN_POSITIVE`, the QR 0.009 at `16 * MIN_POSITIVE`. `norm_l2` returns 0 for a nonzero 4 x 4 matrix with entries about `5e-320`.
  The SVD method and tall QDWH inherit the first two, so the test at the threshold itself covers
  square QDWH only; at `MIN_POSITIVE / eps` everything passes.
- Not done: a benchmark against QR and the SVD-based polar (the issue's last verification item);
  no performance claim is made. QDWH's QR step does not exploit the identity block's structure.
