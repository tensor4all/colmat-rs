# QL and RQ factorizations (colmat-rs#60, PR 1)

## Decisions

- **QL and RQ through reversed views, not a port of `ql.jl` / `rq.jl`.** With
  $P_k$ the reversal permutation of order $k$, the QR factorization
  $P_m A P_n = \tilde Q \tilde R$ gives $A = (P_m \tilde Q P_m)(P_m \tilde R P_n)$,
  where $P_m \tilde R P_n$ is lower trapezoidal and ends in the last row and
  column: the QL factorization. RQ is the same identity applied to LQ. Both
  modules therefore call the existing `qr_in_place` (QL) and `lq_in_place` (RQ)
  on `reverse_rows_and_cols_mut()`, and every extraction routine is the QR or
  LQ one on reversed views. This reuses the blocked, look-ahead and team code
  paths and their validation unchanged, and the packed result has the storage
  layout of LAPACK's `?geqlf` / `?gerqf` (the triangular factor on and below,
  respectively above, the diagonal that ends in the last row and column,
  reflectors in the rest). A port of MatrixFactorizations.jl's reverse
  Householder loops would have duplicated the blocked kernel for no numerical
  gain; no code from that package is used, so `NOTICE` gains no entry. Its
  output is the test oracle.
- **LQ was already carried** (the LQ row in the experiments table, from the
  same faer#130), so PR 1 of the issue reduces to QL and RQ.
- **Positive gauge** is the QR/LQ one applied through the reversal: the
  diagonal that ends in the last row and column of the triangular factor is
  real and nonnegative, and `unitary` applies the matching phase so the product
  is unchanged. With it, a full-rank factorization is unique, which is what the
  later AD rules need.
- **Mirrors the LQ module's surface** (`factor`, `unitary`, `reconstruct`), and
  leaves out `null` and solver-level structs, which the issue did not ask for.

## Verification conclusions and constraints

- `faer/tests/ql_rq.rs`: every output mode into reused buffers at the exact
  scratch size, the trapezoidal zero pattern and the positive diagonal, thin
  factors equal to the trailing columns (QL) or rows (RQ) of the full ones, for
  f32/f64/c32/c64 on empty, square, tall, wide, rank-deficient and graded inputs;
  a row-major input and strided outputs; `Par::Seq` against a two-thread team.
- Against MatrixFactorizations.jl (reference commit
  `a38f07fbbcb8abebb203bf67fc745b8c262d6d19`, f64 and c64, 5x3, 3x5, 4x4):
  thin `Q` and compact triangle agree entry by entry within `200 n eps`. The
  generator is `faer/tests/fixtures/ql_rq_reference/generate.jl`; it applies the
  positive gauge itself, because MatrixFactorizations.jl does not fix it.
- Dropping the gauge in `ql::copy_l` fails the contract and the fixture tests.
- Not done: a LAPACK `?qlt01`/`?rqt01` validation family. The arithmetic is
  the QR/LQ kernel that the `qr` and `lq` families already validate, and the
  reversal itself is covered by the tests above. No allocation test (the
  wrappers allocate nothing of their own; the row-major QR path allocates by
  design, as documented on `qr_in_place`), no performance measurement.
- MatrixFactorizations.jl at the reference commit: `Matrix(F.Q)` of a `QL` gives
  the first $\min(m, n)$ columns of the full `Q` (the generic `AbstractQ` rule),
  which do not reproduce `A` when $m > n$; the thin QL factor is the last
  $\min(m, n)$ columns. Recorded only; upstream feedback is deferred.
